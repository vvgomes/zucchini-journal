# Envy

You were so small.<br/>
You were so tiny.<br/>
Your view was so narrow.<br/>
Your dreams were not ambitious.<br/>
Your joy was simple.<br/>
Then, when you least expected,<br/>
Out of nowhere, he turned up.<br/>
Like a portal.<br/>
He gave you the keys.<br/>
Taught you how to use it.<br/>
He trusted you with heart and soul.<br/>
He was great and growing.<br/>
He knew great people and great things.<br/>
He's been there once or twice.<br/>
Where you'd never imagined yourself at.<br/>
He took you there. He create space for you.<br/>
In the front row. Where most people will never reach.<br/>
He introduced you to them.<br/>
He saw you cry and celebrate.<br/>
He taught you what to say.<br/>
He showed you what to read. What to listen.<br/>
What is good. What isn't.<br/>
And he was even greater.<br/>
In the city that doesn't tolerate less than the very best.<br/>
What he does is fucking hard.<br/>
What he creates if fucking brilliant.<br/>
He has a reputation.<br/>
People hear him.<br/>
People get inspiration from him.<br/>
People feel proud of him.<br/>
He got great things done.<br/>
He got awarded a few times.<br/>
He gave speeches to great audiences.<br/>
Remember who was sitting in the first row.<br/>
He made art that engages people.<br/>
He expressed his art in the biggest city in the world.<br/>
His brilliance. His creativity.<br/>
And he wanted you there. All the way.<br/>

Then, your love became envy.<br/>
Your gratitude turned into hate.<br/>
You became jealous of who he was.<br/>
You wished to destroy him.<br/>
In the worst possible ways one can imagine.<br/>
With all your forces. With your whole body and soul.<br/>
You wished to humiliate him.<br/>
You wanted to diminish him.<br/>
You wanted him to feel small.<br/>
So you can reach his size.<br/>
You gave up on growing.<br/>
And decided to break him instead.<br/>
Because, in reality,<br/>
You're still small.<br/>
You're still tiny.<br/>
You're view is still narrow.<br/>
Your feelings are ugly.<br/>
And you hate the distance between you and him.<br/>
An ever growing distance.<br/>
Since the day you realized,<br/>
That despite his effort and love,<br/>
YOU failed.<br/>
And now you're smaller than ever.<br/>
Just look around yourself.<br/>
Fortunately, he's great enough.<br/>
To survive. And to thrive.<br/>
He will rebuild himself.<br/>
Over and over again.<br/>
However many times he needs to.<br/>
While you fade. Helpless. Struggling. Addicted.<br/>
Like a dying dwarf star.<br/>
Swallowed by its own remains.<br/>
