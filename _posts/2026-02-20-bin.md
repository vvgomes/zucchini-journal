# Bin

I keep checking the bin.<br/>
As if I have ditched something important.<br/>
Something I want to recover now.<br/>
As if I have discarded it on impulse.<br/>
When I was on the edge.<br/>
What exactly do I want to find in the bin?<br/>
Why do I feel the urge to recover it?<br/>
It won't be there. I know that.<br/>
Yet, I keep checking the bin.<br/>
As if it conceals a hidden treasure.<br/>
An answer.<br/>
A meaning.<br/>
An apology.<br/>
