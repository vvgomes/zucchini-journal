# Blind

You were young.<br/>
You were raw.<br/>
You were real.<br/>
You had dreams.<br/>
You had feelings.<br/>
You had an open heart.<br/>
You were special.<br/>
You were nature.<br/>
You were source.<br/>
You were energy.<br/>
You were love.<br/>
But you were blind.<br/>
Unable to see yourself.<br/>
Your whole being.<br/>

...

You were offered a taste.<br/>
The taste of your own blood.<br/>
You got old.<br/>
You got false.<br/>
You no longer dream.<br/>
You no longer feel.<br/>
You closed your heart.<br/>
You blended with them all.<br/>
You became artificial.<br/>
You consumed.<br/>
You desired.<br/>
You hated.<br/>
You pleasured.<br/>
You suffered.<br/>
But you are no longer blind.<br/>
You can finally see yourself.<br/>
Your own remainings.<br/>
