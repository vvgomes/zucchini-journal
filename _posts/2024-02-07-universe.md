# Universe

We plant seeds to the universe<br/>
They might sprout sometimes<br/>
But we learn not to expect anything<br/>
As the universe remains blind, unjust<br/>
There’s pointless suffering<br/>
There’s pointless joy<br/>
We get angry at times, we get tired<br/>
We want to fight the universe back<br/>
Though there’s only one way to hurt the universe<br/>
We hurt the universe by denying it<br/>
When we refrain from being part of it<br/>
When we refuse donating life to it<br/>
When we leave the universe behind for good<br/>
We can only hurt the universe by ceasing to exist<br/>
