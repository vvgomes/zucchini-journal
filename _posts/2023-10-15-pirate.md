# Pirate

Pirate drives a school bus outta town<br/>
Sailing through the mild depressive crowd<br/>
Starting on Monday<br/>
Everything’s the same<br/>
Suicide thoughts hanging by the shroud<br/>

Pirate wants you feeling good tonight<br/>
Humanized perception of the lights<br/>
Bertrand Russel son<br/>
Getting nothing done<br/>
Heavy suitcase many flights of stairs<br/>

Classic cowboy writes about the past<br/>
White hat cobra languishing so fast<br/>
Sending love and hate<br/>
Someone to be blamed<br/>
Eye-patch color blindness underdressed<br/>

Pirate drives a stolen motor boat<br/>
Trades illegal goods with Billy Goat<br/>
Purify his mind<br/>
Nineteen eighty five<br/>
Green tea for two lovers you don’t know<br/>

Pirate tries to keep his low profile<br/>
Friend has sharp knives, enemy has doubts<br/>
Ego death at night<br/>
Jet boots feel so high<br/>
Sidewalk street light glowing your blackout<br/>

Concrete tingling legs will keep you stuck<br/>
Grotesque ego owners can’t do drugs<br/>
Pirate’s not a friend<br/>
Won’t see you again<br/>
Silent heart in point of no return<br/>

Pirate comes back, offers you a dream<br/>
Laughs in overwhelming self-esteem<br/>
Sea storm won’t be long<br/>
Distant old folk song<br/>
Years later waking up downstream<br/>

Doctor sends you home as you may die<br/>
Pirate writes a letter in despise<br/>
You have passed away<br/>
On a sunny day<br/>
Anxious lover waiting for next time<br/>
