# Year

I didn't write much during 2025.<br/>
No inspiration, no motivation.<br/>
I have had some unfortunate years recently.<br/>
But 2025 was just empty.<br/>
I was just going through the motions.<br/>
I let others hurt me. I let others drain me.<br/>
So I could have others around me.<br/>
Now I'm back to writing. Back by myself.<br/>
Lonely and with space to think.<br/>
