# Ugly

You became ugly<br/>
Ugly and false<br/>
False and selfish<br/>
Selfish and impulsive<br/>
Impulsive and immediate<br/>
Immediate and careless<br/>
Careless and forgetful<br/>
Forgetful and ruthless<br/>
Ruthless and steady<br/>
Steady and silent<br/>
Silent and cold<br/>
Cold and pale<br/>
Pale and rigid<br/>
Rigid and dry<br/>
Dry and motionless<br/>
Motionless and timeless<br/>
Timeless and forgotten<br/>
Forgotten and dead<br/>
Dead and no longer ugly<br/>
